Questions

Will replies get removed?

Some might. Moderators decide, and nobody can promise they won't. We read each community's rules first, skip the ones that ban product mentions, and mark every removal on your report so you can see what happened. On Scale we replace removed replies.

Whose account posts the replies?

By default, ours, with a line naming your business. You can also have us post from your own account. At Scale volumes we recommend your account (or a few from your team), since one account posting dozens of times a day looks like spam.

Do people know it's an ad?

Yes. Every reply ends with a line saying it's posted on behalf of your product. Hidden promotion breaks Reddit's rules and gets products banned. Disclosed, useful replies tend to hold up much better.

How do you find the threads?

We search Reddit for posts asking for recommendations, alternatives to tools you compete with, or help with the problem you solve. Each one is checked for fit before anyone writes a word.

Who writes the replies?

AI drafts each reply from the thread and your product details. A person reads every draft, fixes it if needed, and posts it. Nothing goes up unchecked.

What if my niche is too small to hit the total?

We keep posting after the end date until you reach it. If there just aren't enough fitting threads, we'll refund the unused part.

Is this a subscription?

No. Every plan is a single payment. Nothing renews. Buy again any time.
